Client questions

Frequently asked questions

We already have extinguishers. Why do we need a hydrant system?

Extinguishers are for small, early-stage fires. A hydrant gives continuous high-pressure water for large fires and a ready connection for the fire brigade. For most commercial, institutional and industrial buildings it is also an NBC and Fire NOC requirement.

Will all sprinklers go off and flood the building?

No. Each head has its own heat-sensitive glass bulb — only the head(s) exposed to the fire's heat open. The rest stay closed.

How much does it cost?

It depends on area, height, hazard class, number of points, pipe sizes, pump room and tank. After a FREE site survey we give you a detailed BOQ and a transparent quotation. Call 77720 78610 or fill the form.

Another vendor quoted lower. Why should we pay more?

Compare the scope, not just the total: pipe class, pump specification, material make, pump room work, testing, handover documents and after-sales support. If something is missing from a lower quote, it usually comes back later as extra cost, rework or delay. We give you an item-wise BOQ so the comparison is clear.

Our drawing is not final yet. Can you still help?

Yes. A final BOQ needs a final drawing, but after a site survey we can review your requirement and share a budgetary estimate and planning inputs — such as pipe routes, sleeves and pump room space — so nothing is missed during construction.

Can you guarantee the Fire NOC?

No honest vendor can — the NOC is issued by the fire department and depends on the whole building. What we do is design, install, test and document our scope properly, and support you during the inspection.

Do you help with Fire NOC?

Yes. We design and document the system as per NBC and local fire norms, and provide drawings, test reports and handover documents for your Fire NOC file. Final approval is issued by the fire department.
