Client questions

Frequently asked questions

We already have fire extinguishers. Why do we need a hydrant system?

Extinguishers are for small, early-stage fires. A hydrant system gives continuous, high-pressure water for large fires and a ready connection for the fire brigade. For most commercial, institutional and industrial buildings it is also an NBC and Fire NOC requirement.

We have a hydrant. Why do we also need sprinklers?

A hydrant needs people to operate it. Sprinklers respond automatically and start controlling the fire at its first stage — even at night or when the area is empty — often before the fire brigade arrives.

Will all sprinklers go off and flood the building?

No. Each head has its own heat-sensitive glass bulb. Only the head(s) exposed to the fire's heat open — the rest stay closed.

How much does a hydrant or sprinkler system cost?

It depends on area, height, hazard class, number of hydrant points or sprinklers, pipe sizes, pump room and tank. After a FREE site survey we give you a detailed BOQ and a transparent quotation. Call 77720 78610 or fill the form.

Do you help with Fire NOC?

Yes. We design and document the system as per NBC and local fire norms, and provide drawings, test reports and handover documents for your Fire NOC file, along with documentation support. Final approval is issued by the fire department.

How long does installation take?

It depends on pipe quantity, building size, site readiness and material availability. You get a project schedule with the quotation, plus regular progress photos.

Can you repair or upgrade our existing system?

Yes. We check pressure, leakage, pump auto-mode and coverage gaps, then repair, extend or upgrade the system — and can maintain it under an AMC.
