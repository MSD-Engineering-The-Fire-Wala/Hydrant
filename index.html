Hydrant or Sprinkler?

Which system does your building need?

They do different jobs. Our engineers assess your occupancy, height, area and fire load and recommend only what your site actually needs.

Fire Hydrant

Manual firefighting — for large fires

  • Continuous high-pressure water across the premises
  • Operated by trained staff & the fire brigade
  • Covers yards, floors, basements & perimeters
  • Extinguishers handle small fires — hydrants handle big ones

Fire Sprinkler

Automatic suppression — stops fire at stage one

  • Works automatically, 24×7, even when no one is there
  • Controls fire before it spreads — buys evacuation time
  • Only the affected area gets water
  • Improves insurance risk grading

Most hospitals, hotels, malls, warehouses and high-rise buildings need both. We design them together and integrate both with your fire pump room and water tank — one team, one responsibility.

Common questions

Hydrant & sprinkler FAQs

We already have fire extinguishers. Why do we need a hydrant system?

Extinguishers are meant for small, early-stage fires. A hydrant system provides continuous, high-pressure water for large fires and gives the fire brigade a ready connection to your premises. For most commercial, institutional and industrial buildings it is also an NBC and Fire NOC requirement.

We have a hydrant system. Why do we also need sprinklers?

A hydrant needs people to operate it. A sprinkler system responds automatically — it starts controlling the fire at its initial stage, even at night or when the area is empty, often before the fire brigade arrives. Together they give you both automatic and manual protection.

Will all sprinklers go off and flood the building?

No. Each sprinkler head has its own heat-sensitive glass bulb. Only the head(s) directly exposed to the fire's heat open — the rest stay closed. Fire damage is almost always far more expensive than the limited water used.

How much does a hydrant or sprinkler system cost?

Cost depends on your building's area, height, hazard class, number of hydrant points or sprinklers, pipe sizes, pump room and tank requirements. After a site survey we give you a detailed BOQ and transparent quotation — no guesswork. Fill the form above or call 77720 78610 to get started.

Do you help with Fire NOC?

Yes. We design and document the system as per NBC and local fire authority norms, and provide all test reports, drawings and handover documents needed for your Fire NOC file, along with NOC documentation and technical compliance support. The final approval is issued by the fire department.

How long does installation take?

Timelines depend on pipe quantity, building size, site readiness and material availability. We share a project schedule with your quotation and keep you updated with regular progress photos and milestone reports.

Can you repair or upgrade our existing system?

Yes. We review existing hydrant and sprinkler systems for pressure, leakage, pump auto-mode and coverage gaps, then repair, extend or upgrade them — and can maintain them afterwards under an AMC.
